Privacy Policy for SensorTracker: Sensors Logging

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

User privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the SensorTracker: Sensors Logging mobile application handles user data.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

The SensorTracker application does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal data, sensitive data, or device sensor data with third parties.

All processes related to reading and writing data from sensors (such as accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, etc.) take place exclusively locally on the user's device. The application does not have internet connectivity features to transmit measurement data.

3. On-device Data Usage (Local Storage)

SensorTracker allows exporting sensor readings. The results of these measurements are saved as CSV files directly in the public Downloads folder on the user's device, to which the user has full access. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy or share the CSV file yourself.

4. Required Permissions

The application may request access to the following permissions, which are strictly necessary for its proper local operation:

  • Access to high sampling rate sensors (HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS) - Required to retrieve accurate, detailed physical measurements (above 200 Hz) from the built-in hardware sensors.

5. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at the email address provided in the application's developer profile on the Google Play Store.
